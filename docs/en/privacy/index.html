FundTime · iPhone

Privacy policy

FundTime keeps its records on your iPhone. This policy describes the app, its local extensions, purchases and information you choose to send when contacting support.

Updated 10 October 2026

Local records

Budgets, withdrawals, term deposits, history and preferences are stored locally. The app and its extensions share only the snapshots and events needed for control, widgets and local notifications through an iOS App Group. FundTime has no user account, developer server, advertising, analytics service or cloud sync. It does not send time records or device usage to the developer.

Screen Time authorisation

FundTime uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity frameworks to manage the apps and categories you select. Apple provides private selection tokens. FundTime does not read messages, browsing content or what you do inside selected apps. You can change your selection or revoke authorisation in iOS Settings to stop control.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. You can choose notification types in FundTime or turn off notifications in iOS Settings. Widgets display remaining minutes and related states from local snapshots and do not connect to a developer server.

Trial and purchases

Apple handles trial and purchase transactions through the App Store. FundTime reads verified purchase status and the trial start date, and does not receive payment card details. A copy of the trial start date is stored in this iPhone’s Keychain so reinstalling does not restart the trial. Apple may restore transactions linked to your Apple Account. Apple’s handling of data is covered by its privacy policy.

Removal and retention

Removing FundTime deletes its local app records. The trial start date in Keychain and App Store transactions may remain; removing the app does not delete Apple’s purchase records. FundTime provides no cloud account or sync service.

Support email

The developer receives your email address, message and attachments only when you choose to email support. They are used to respond to and resolve your request and are handled by an email service. Avoid sending passwords, payment details, app selections or unnecessary private information. You may email to request deletion of support messages you submitted. Requests about transaction records held by Apple must be directed to Apple.

This website

This website provides support and privacy information. It has no added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts or forms. GitHub Pages may process technical connection information to deliver and protect this website. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from the local time records in the FundTime app.

Changes and contact

If the app’s handling of data changes, this policy will be updated with a new date. Contact the email address below with privacy questions or support requests.
