FundTime · iPhone

Support

Help with daily budgets, the trial, purchases and app blocking.

Plan, save and withdraw the minutes you use in entertainment and social apps. FundTime is for iPhone and requires iOS 26 or later and Screen Time authorisation.

How do I get started?

Complete setup in FundTime, choose a daily budget for each weekday and a settlement time, then select the apps or categories to manage. Once you start the trial or purchase full access, selected apps are blocked. Preview and withdraw minutes in FundTime before using them. When those minutes are used, iOS applies the block again.

How does the free trial work?

FundTime is free to download. After setup, tap Start 7-day trial to create a free App Store transaction and begin the trial. All features are available for seven days, with no automatic charge. The original verified transaction date and a local Keychain copy record when the trial began; reinstalling does not restart it.

What happens when the trial ends?

Without a full unlock purchase, all FundTime blocks are removed and daily settlement stops. Savings, term deposits and history stay on this iPhone. Full access is a single purchase, with no subscription, and supports Family Sharing. The App Store purchase sheet shows the applicable price.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then tap Restore purchases on the trial or purchase screen. Check that your iPhone is online. If no record is found, check your App Store purchase history and the purchase sharing settings for your family.

Why is a selected app not blocked as expected?

Check that Screen Time authorisation for FundTime is still active, then check your selection and today’s control status. iOS may take time to apply a block. Open FundTime to refresh its state. If the problem continues, contact support with your iOS and FundTime versions and the time it occurred.

How do I stop FundTime’s control?

You can revoke FundTime’s Screen Time authorisation in iOS Settings. FundTime removes its control after revocation. All blocks are also removed when the trial expires without a full unlock purchase.

Can I change notifications and language?

FundTime settings let you choose notification types and select System, Traditional Chinese (Hong Kong) or English. Notification permission can also be changed in iOS Settings. The app language is shared with notifications, shields and widgets.

Are my records synced or backed up to a cloud service?

FundTime has no account, server or cloud sync service. Its time ledger and preferences stay on your iPhone. Removing the app deletes its local app records; the trial date in Keychain and App Store transactions may remain. Avoid reinstalling as your first troubleshooting step, as local records may be lost.

Contact support

Include your FundTime version, iOS version, when the issue occurred and the steps to reproduce it. You may include a screenshot after removing private information. Do not send your ledger, app selection, Apple Account password or payment details.
